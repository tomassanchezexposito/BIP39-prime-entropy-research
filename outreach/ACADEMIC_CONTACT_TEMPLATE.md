# Academic / Research Group Contact Template

## Subject

Independent reproducible research on BIP-39 entropy and deterministic prime-coordinate mappings

## Message

Dear Professor / Research Group,

I am contacting you to share an independent computational research project that may be relevant to your work in cryptography, number theory, cybersecurity, distributed systems, or reproducible research.

The project studies:

- BIP-39 entropy and SHA-256 checksum construction
- deterministic mappings between BIP-39 positions and ordinal odd primes
- finite and absolute-coordinate representations
- deterministic structural rejection filters
- Bitcoin HD-wallet derivation tooling
- reproducibility and machine-readable research datasets

An important limitation is explicit throughout the project: prime-number mappings, coordinate transformations, and deterministic filters are not treated as sources of additional cryptographic entropy.

The repository includes Python source code, automated tests, technical architecture, a reproducibility protocol, public experimental datasets, and a citable Zenodo software release.
