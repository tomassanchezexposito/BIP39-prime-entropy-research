# Zenodo Communities Guide

## Why use communities

Zenodo Communities are curated spaces for projects, institutions, domains, and conferences.

A published record can be submitted to a community for inclusion.

Community inclusion does not change the project's scientific claims and should not be described as endorsement unless the community explicitly says so.

## Submission workflow

For the published v1.1.0 record:

1. open the Zenodo record
2. click the community/settings cog-wheel
3. choose `Submit to community`
4. search for a genuinely relevant community
5. inspect its scope and curation policy
6. select the community
7. grant the required curator view/edit access
8. optionally add a short curator message
9. submit
10. monitor the pending submission

Repeat individually for additional relevant communities.

## Selection rule

Only submit when the record clearly fits the community scope.

Good matches may involve:

- Bitcoin / blockchain research
- cryptography
- research software
- open science / reproducibility
- Python scientific software
- computational mathematics

Avoid mass-submitting to loosely related communities.
